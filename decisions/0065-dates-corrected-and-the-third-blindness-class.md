## 2. T1 — the third blindness class

**`second-brain` found that its own memory carried `0054` §3's margin argument as live supporting
reasoning in three files for nine entries**, after `0055` §2 withdrew it.

The argument: *"the 90 have p5 margin 1.7 days and a minimum of 0.13 — demonstrably alive for roughly 89
of the 91 days, had full opportunity to generate the evidence, did not."* **Withdrawn as cherry-picked**
— the same 90 have **median 44.5** (instance B reproduced p5 = 1.6552, median = 44.5272), and the
correct ground **carries no margin statistic at all**: a floor is a worst case, admissibility sets an
endpoint, plausibility does not enter.

**Why neither control could ever have seen it, and this is the point:**

| Half | What it matches | Why it misses this |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **numeric** | superseded **values**, at both precisions | **`1.7` and `0.13` are correct statistics.** They were withdrawn as *grounds*, not as figures. There is no superseded number |
| **phrase** | withdrawn **claims**, verbatim fragments | the argument travels **paraphrased**, not quoted. There is no stable string |

**So the class is: a withdrawn ARGUMENT built from CORRECT STATISTICS.** It is recognised by what it
*claims*, not by its digits or its text. **Both existing halves are blind to it by construction, not by
oversight.**

**It is the B8 shape on surface 7** — B8 was a withdrawn claim the numeric half could not see; this is a
withdrawn ground neither half can see. **And it was found by a reading agent, not by a control**, which
is the same way the covering qualifier's absence was found at `0062` §4.

## 3. No control is built for it, deliberately

**A checker for this would be a prose checker**, and the phrase half is explicitly *"not a general prose
checker and does not pretend to be"* (`0061` §3). **Building one tonight would add machinery of a kind
this chain has repeatedly shipped broken** — three controls in six entries reported clean while checking
nothing.

**What stands in for one, and it costs nothing:** when an entry withdraws a **ground** rather than a
**figure**, it says so in the withdrawn-claims register and **names the statistics that remain true but
are no longer load-bearing.** The statistic is not wrong; the *use* of it is. `second-brain` has already
added this shape to its register as **Mode H**.

**Recorded in `CLAUDE.md` as the third of three blindness classes**, two checked and one not.
